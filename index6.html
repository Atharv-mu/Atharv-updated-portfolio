From Setbacks to Stability: My Journey to CSE Core

Personal Journey

My primary goal was simple to get into a government engineering college. I worked really hard for it and during counselling I came very close. At the college-level counselling stage, I truly believed I was going to make it. But it didn't happen. That moment hit me hard. I felt disappointed, sad, and honestly a bit lost. It felt like all my effort had led to a dead end. Since my rank wasn't high enough, I decided not to drop a year and instead joined another private college. I took admission in Robotics and Automation, even though deep down I knew it wasn't the branch I truly wanted. My real goal had always been Computer Science Engineering (CSE Core). After one month, with my father's support, I requested the college for a branch change to CSE. They didn't approve it because my percentage was low, but they allowed me to move to Information Technology instead. It wasn't my dream branch, but it felt like a small step closer. Even then, my goal never changed. I decided to stay patient, work on myself, and wait for another opportunity. The whole year felt like a test of patience. There were moments of frustration, self-doubt, and uncertainty, but I kept going. Finally, after a long wait, I got another chance and this time I made it to CSE Core. This journey wasn't just about changing a branch. It was about handling disappointment, staying patient, and continuing even when things don't go as planned. Sometimes life doesn't give you what you want immediately, but if you stay consistent and don't give up, you eventually reach where you're meant to be.
